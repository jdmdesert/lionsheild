# Lion Shield Barbershop — Airo rebuild brief

Create a responsive, informational website for **Lion Shield Barbershop**. Do not add booking, appointments, checkout, customer accounts, forms, or ecommerce.

## Visual direction

- Premium but friendly neighborhood barbershop.
- White navigation header with a crowned lion shield logo and bold condensed **LION SHIELD / BARBERSHOP** wordmark.
- Black, white, warm gold, vivid blue, and small red accents.
- Bold condensed headings, clean sans-serif body copy, strong spacing, and crisp dividers.
- Large photographic hero featuring a black leather barber chair inside a warm, dark, modern barbershop.
- Hero copy: **Your Neighborhood Barbershop**.
- Supporting line: **Classic cuts. Modern styles. Friendly faces.**
- An outlined **Our Services** anchor button that scrolls down the page. It must not book an appointment.

## Page structure

1. Sticky header: Home, Services, Our Barbers, Gallery, Contact.
2. Hero: headline, supporting line, and non-transactional Services anchor.
3. Quick service strip: Haircuts / Classic & Modern; Beard Trims / Shape & Maintain; Our Barbers / Experienced & Friendly.
4. Services section: Classic Haircut, Skin Fade, Beard Trim, Kids' Cut.
5. Our Barbers section focused on precision, conversation, and community without inventing staff names.
6. Gallery section using the supplied barbershop and barber-at-work images.
7. Informational contact section for verified address, hours, phone number, email, and social links once those details are supplied.
8. Footer with the brand and the line **Classic cuts. Modern styles. Friendly faces.**

## Content rules

- Do not invent an address, phone number, email, business hours, pricing, staff names, testimonials, awards, or social media URLs.
- Use clearly labeled placeholders for any business details that have not been confirmed.
- Keep the site informational only.
- Optimize the layout for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
